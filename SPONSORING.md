# Support StackChan AI Server / 支持项目

Sponsorship is **entirely voluntary**. You may use, modify and commercially
redistribute the software without donating, subject to its applicable license.
Donations do not waive source-availability obligations or buy copyright,
exclusive rights, priority support, promised features or a commercial exception.
Paid support or custom development, if offered, requires a separate agreement.

Support helps fund maintenance, device testing, AI-provider compatibility tests,
and packaging/signing work. Contributions of bug reports, documentation,
translations and testing are equally welcome. Sponsors are acknowledged publicly
only with their consent. Do not assume charitable tax deductibility.

## Payment destinations

**No verified payment destination is published yet.** A maintainer-confirmed
public PayPal link can be added here and to GitHub's Sponsor button later. Do not
send money to payment details copied from unrelated issues, comments or forks.

Cryptocurrency is optional, subject to local rules and explicit confirmation of
the currency, network, public receiving address and memo/tag if required. Public
blockchain payments can expose transaction relationships. Never share private
keys, recovery phrases or account passwords. No payment is needed to use this
project, and the project never asks you to connect a wallet to unlock features.
