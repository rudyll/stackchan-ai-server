# Contributing / 参与贡献

Thank you for contributing fixes, documentation, testing or translations.
Please discuss substantial changes in an issue first and include relevant tests.
Never submit API keys, tokens, private wallet material or personal device data.

## License and ownership

New contributions are accepted under **AGPL-3.0-only**, the current project
license, unless explicitly agreed otherwise before acceptance. You must have the
right to contribute the code. Identify reused material and keep its original
copyright/license notices; do not label third-party work as your own.

You retain copyright in your contribution. Submitting a contribution does not
assign copyright, grant a blanket proprietary-relicensing right, or require a
donation. There is no copyright-assignment CLA. If a future commercial exception
needs permissions beyond the existing licenses, those permissions must be
obtained separately from the relevant rights holders.

AGPL source availability does not require a downstream developer to submit a PR
to this repository. Upstream contributions and sponsorship are welcome but
voluntary. See [copyright notices](stackchan-server/NOTICE.md),
[licensing policy](docs/licensing.md) and [sponsorship](SPONSORING.md).

## 中文

欢迎修复、文档、测试和翻译贡献。较大改动请先开 issue 讨论，并补充相关测试。
请勿提交密钥、令牌、钱包私钥/助记词或私人设备资料。

新增贡献默认以 **AGPL-3.0-only** 提供；如有例外，须在接纳前明确约定。你必须有权
提交相关代码，注明复用来源，并保留上游的版权与许可声明。

贡献者保留版权；提交代码不等于转让版权，也不自动授权维护者把你的贡献改成
专有商业许可。没有强制版权转让 CLA，没有强制捐赠。未来若商业授权需要超出
现有许可证的权限，必须另行取得相关权利人的同意。

下游按 AGPL 提供对应源码，不等于必须向本仓库提交 PR；回馈代码与赞助均自愿。
